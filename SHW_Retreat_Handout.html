The Inheritance

This is not a fresh start. It is a response.

Many of the conditions named in this year's survey were named publicly five years ago. The work since 2022 has been the response to a documented institutional inheritance — Keeling 2021, SHC space documented since 2013, SMHF rolled into tuition last fiscal year, and zero cuts to SH&W in the 25%/15% budget exercise.

Where the Work Hasn't Reached Yet

The outer ring · institutional policy and law · seven open gaps
Inner rings reached. Outer ring, not yet. Using the social-ecological model: this division has built at the individual, interpersonal, and organizational levels. What is still pending is institutional policy embedding — the kind of change that requires a seat at tables we have not consistently been at.
