Getting to Know Each Other
Department Tours & a Picture Org Chart
Student Health & Wellness spans multiple centers, buildings, and specialties. That range is our strength — and it also means it's easy to work alongside people whose names you know but whose work you've never seen. We're changing that with two new initiatives, both led by Cha McNeil.
Department tours. We'll be visiting each department in its own space. You'll get to see where the work happens, meet the team in person, and hear directly from them about what they do and who they serve. Tours will be scheduled across the year so everyone has a chance to attend.
A picture org chart. We're building a visual directory of the division — each staff member's photo, title, role, and three short bullets describing the main things they do. No more guessing who to call. When you need something, you'll know exactly who does that work and what they look like when you pass them in the hall.
Where it lives, and why. You'll submit your photo to a secure Box folder, and that same folder is where the finished picture org chart will be housed. We've chosen Box rather than a public page as a safety precaution — this directory is for our team, and keeping it access-controlled protects staff whose roles make public visibility a concern.
Why it matters. Coordinated care starts with knowing your colleagues. When you can put a face to a name and understand what a team actually handles, referrals get warmer, hand-offs get smoother, and students feel the difference.
What's next. Watch this space — Cha will share the Box folder link and the department tour schedule shortly. In the meantime, start thinking about the three things you'd want your colleagues to know about your role.
Led by Cha McNeil — details coming soon

Survey Results
Post-Retreat Survey Results — Pre-to-Post Movement
43 staff completed the post-retreat survey. Mission Connection moved from 3.48 → 4.09 (+0.61). Strategic Roadmap Awareness moved from 2.82 → 4.05 (+1.23) — the largest single change in the dataset. 67% said the retreat made them more comfortable reaching across teams. The pre-survey identified the gap as "a translation problem, not a strategy problem" — the retreat translated it. Full staff report available via the Reports section.

Fall Projects
Fall 2026 Division Projects — What's in Motion
Six workstreams are active across the division. Success360 begins with Fidelity in the fall alongside upcoming staff training. Active work is underway to identify division-wide capacity — staffing, scheduling, space, and utilization — across every department.
